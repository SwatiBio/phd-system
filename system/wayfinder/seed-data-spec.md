## 1. Joining & Registration — `reg + 3 days` 🔴

**Exact actions (Section 5, Annexures 11/12, 13–18):**
1. Admission order comes after fee payment + document verification. Visit **Admissions Office with originals**: all semester/year marksheets of Bachelor's · all of Master's · UG & PG degree certificates · 2 recent identical passport-size photos · self-attested PAN + Aadhaar photocopies · fee-paid details
2. Complete joining/reporting formalities at the institution → **ID card + learner email** issued
3. Submit **joining report to CDS within 3 days of joining**: **Annexure 12** (Dr. TMA Pai joining report) + **undertaking Annexure 14** (TMA Pai)
4. CDS issues **registration letter** → KEEP SAFE; it is the reference for all future communications and carries your semester-wise timelines
5. **Date of joining = date of registration** — the anchor for everything below

## 2. Register Coursework — `reg + 1 week` 🟡

**Exact actions (Section 13):**
- Register **mandatory courses immediately after registration**
- Courses must be **Masters level or higher**, recommended by DAC, with assessment
- **12 credits total, completed by end of semester 2** (deficits flagged at DAC-1; unresolved before DAC-2)
- Online/training courses *without* assessment need guide/department evaluation, reported at next DAC

## 3. DAC Constitution + Preliminary DAC — `reg + 1 mo` / `reg + 2 mo` 🟡→🔴

**Constitution (Section 9, Annexure 22):** committee members list (template 22), signed by **guide + HOI**
**Preliminary DAC (within 2 months, Section 12.2):**
- DAC **ratifies coursework + topic/objectives**; reviews regulatory/ethics requirements
- Minutes recorded on **Annexure 23**, filed at institution
- Outcome feeds IPAC submission directly

## 4. IPAC Protocol Presentation — `per registration letter (~sem 1)` 🔴

**Documents to PhD office (Section 10):**
- DAC members list (22) signed · Preliminary DAC minutes (23) signed by HOI/Chairman · interim minutes if any · checklist (24) signed by scholar + guide · EC/IAEC/Bio-safety approvals **or evidence of submission** · protocol PDF (25) **approved and signed by guide**
- After approval: coordinator sends minutes (27) + members list (22) + checklist (24) + final protocol to CDS within 5 days; CDS confirms within 15 days

**Protocol itself (Annexure 25) — 15 MANDATORY sections:**
Title + scholar name/affiliation · Guide/co-guide name/affiliation · Introduction · Literature Review · Research Gaps identified · Objectives · Detailed Methodology · Expected Outcome · Importance + link to SDGs · Research Time Plan · Pilot study/preliminary work · **Expenses break-up + funding source (scholarship must NOT be listed)** · References · DAC-suggested coursework with credits · **Similarity check report: total word count, similarity index <15%, exclude matches <3 words, signed by scholar + guide**

**Format:** 15–20 pages, A4, 1-inch margins
**Approval date rule:** presentation date if approved as-is; date final revised proposal submitted, if modifications were requested

## 5. Semester Renewal — `reg + 6 mo, +12 mo, … (before each semester)` 🟡

**Exact actions (Section 6, Annexure 19):**
- Renewal form (19) **with guide approval**, based on: (a) status of work done, (b) **no dues from MAHE finance**
- **Independent of DAC/IPAC meetings** — never skip it because a DAC is pending
- **Not renewed → NOT on roll for that semester**
- TMA Pai consequence for late renewal: **scholarship withheld until renewed + duration extended by the delay period**
- Renewal also applies during extension period

## 6. Half-yearly DAC + Progress Report — `reg + 12 mo, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42` 🟡→🔴

**Report (Annexure 28, Sections 11–12):**
- 2000–2500 words, explanatory, covering **previous 6 months**
- Guide-approved → shared with DAC members **7 working days before meeting** (portal locks after due date)
- ≥4 DAC members accept → meeting scheduled; scholar presents
- Approved report + minutes to CDS within 10 days; **never combine reports**
- Minimum **6 DACs within 3.5 years**; DAC-1 (end sem 2) reviews coursework status
- Graded per rubric (Annexure 29): poor/very-poor → interim DAC within 3 months + scholarship withheld

**Also required at every DAC (Section 19 "other requirements"):** attendance recorded on all working days (9am–5pm workstation) · logbook current

## 7. WEEKLY LOGBOOK — `every week` (MAHE MANDATE, Section 19) 🟡

> "Maintain research logbook recording PhD related tasks performed on daily basis including status, results, pending research questions/objectives, status of course work and other PhD requirements. **To be submitted to PhD supervisor on weekly basis for review and approval** — serves as official record of contact. Also submitted before seeking NOC."

**Implication:** the daily log IS a compliance document. The weekly review must produce a clean, guide-reviewable log excerpt (the week's tagged entries). Before thesis NOC, the full logbook is submitted — so it must be complete from day 1.

## 8. Publication Milestones (Section 19)

| Milestone | Deadline | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Draft 1 → guide | end sem 2 (`reg + 12 mo`) | review OR original article from thesis research |
| Guide-approved final 1 | end sem 3 (`reg + 18 mo`) | — |
| Paper 1 → journal | end sem 4 (`reg + 24 mo`) | — |
| Draft 2 → guide | end sem 5 (`reg + 30 mo`) | second article from thesis |

**Mandatory for synopsis/thesis:** 2 first-author papers in SCOPUS/WoS (JCR), related to thesis subject, published/accepted **before** synopsis + thesis submission — one may be a review, the other MUST be original from thesis. **OR shortcut:** 1 first-author original in Q1 journal. MAHE affiliation advisory applies; two co-first-authors → only one can claim it.
**Conferences:** 2 paper/poster presentations at reputed national/international conferences **with certificates**, before thesis adjudication. Predatory conferences forbidden.

## 9. Synopsis Presentation — `after final DAC-6, sem 7 (~reg + 42 mo)` 🔴

**Exact actions (Section 20, Annexures 32/33):**
- Synopsis in **Annexure 32 format**; presentation = **1-hour PPT**, scheduled with **1 week notice**, open to all faculty + students
- Scheduled ONLY when submission requirements are met; **DAC participation mandatory**
- Verdict: **APPROVED / MINOR REVISIONS (guide reviews revised synopsis) / MAJOR REVISIONS (DAC sets resubmission timeline)**
- Minutes in **Annexure 33**
- Thesis NOC from CDS within 3 months of synopsis

## 10. Thesis NOC + Submission — `synopsis + ≤3 mo` 🔴

**NOC documents (Section 22):** Turnitin/similarity report of whole thesis (word count, index <15%, "less than 3 words" option, bibliography + quotes excluded, first-author papers in annexures excludable) · word-count undertaking (**Annexure 40**) if thesis <30,000 words · publication details (**Annexure 36**) · **2 conference presentation certificates** · consolidated coursework grade sheet (Registrar) · synopsis proceedings (33) · **dues clearance certificate** (sfin.mahe@manipal.edu)
**Submission to Registrar (Evaluation):** synopsis PDF ≤24 pages (32) · thesis PDF ≤10MB/file (37 sections incl. cover 38, certificate 39, structured abstract ≤2000 words, declaration 35, chapters per DAC, bibliography style per DAC, publication list, biodata) · A4, 1-inch margins
