# Milestones — MAHE PhD (Dr. TMA Pai · 3.5 yrs)

**Every date here is an offset from `reg`.** Nothing below stores a calendar date — the app computes them, so changing `reg:` in the CMS moves the whole timeline at once. Read it at `/timeline.html`.

**Grammar the app understands:** `**Target:** reg + N day|week|month|year` for one date, `**Targets:** reg + ... · reg + ...` for a series. Keep that shape when editing.

**🟡 = prep starts · 🔴 = urgent (7 days).** Rule sources: `PHDOS-DOC-1` (full MAHE rules + rubric).

**Target:** reg + 3 days

- **Submit joining report to CDS within 3 days** — Annexure 12 (TMA Pai) + undertaking (Annexure 14). ⚠️ Both forms are NOT on the MAHE annexures page — request them from CDS.

## 5 · IPAC Protocol — 🔴 reg + 6 months
**Target:** reg + 6 months
- **The guidelines fix this at the sixth month after registration**; the registration letter may give an exact date — it wins
- **Submit document set:** members list (22) · prelim minutes (23) · interim minutes (23, if any) · checklist (24) · EC/IAEC/biosafety approval or submission evidence · protocol PDF (**Annexure 25**, guide-signed)
- **Protocol: 15 mandatory sections** — 15–20 pages, A4, 1" margins, similarity <15% (<3-word matches excluded), signed
- **Expenses break-up — scholarship NOT listed**
- ⚠️ **TMA Pai delay penalty: scholarship withheld until approval AND duration extended by the delay**
