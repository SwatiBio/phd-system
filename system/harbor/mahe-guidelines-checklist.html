MAHE PhD — What You Need To Do

Guidelines-driven checklist for a Dr. TMA Pai Scholarship scholar (Full-Time, T&S stream) · 3.5-year minimum
Phases 1–5 = actions ☑ = track & tick off Keep in mind = rules that bite

Phase 1 Joining & Registration

Day 0 → within days of joining
  • Complete joining formalities at your institution after the admission office confirms your documents.
    You're here now — waiting for the email to start the joining-report procedure.
  • Get your ID card & learner email ID issued after joining.
  • Submit the joining report to CDS within 3 days of joining (Annexure 11/12 + undertaking 13–18).
    Tip: the date of joining is your registration date — this starts your clock.
  • Keep your registration letter as reference for all future communication with CDS/guide/HOD.
    It lists your course requirements, fee timeline, and 6-month semester timelines.

Phase 2 First Semester (months 1–6)

The formalities cluster — most time-sensitive phase
  • Register for mandatory coursework immediately after registration.
  • Doctoral Advisory Committee (DAC) constituted within ~1 month of registration.
  • Preliminary DAC meeting within ~2 months — finalize research topic + coursework plan.
  • Submit guide-approved research protocol for IPAC as per your registration letter's timeline.
    IPAC = Institutional Protocol Approval Committee. Approval date matters — it anchors your research timeline.
  • Register for the next semester / renew registration at end of semester one.
  • Start your literature review track — read your guide's prior lichen papers early (your own goal).

Phase 3 Ongoing — every 6 months

The recurring cadence of your whole PhD
  • Half-yearly progress report to DAC (Annexure 28) — semester-wise, physical presence required.
  • DAC meeting presentation and grading against the scholar progress rubric (Annexure 29).
  • Registration renewal each semester (Annexure 19).

Phase 4 Pre-Thesis Requirements

Mandatory before you can submit — plan these into your years 2–3
  • Mandatory publications (journal output) as per MAHE requirements — track these from day one.
  • Conference presentation (paper or poster) — national/international.
  • Synopsis presentation (Annexure 32/33) — formal proposal of the thesis.
  • Check publication list template (Annexure 36) so your papers feed the thesis cleanly.

Phase 5 Thesis & Defense (final 6 months)

The endgame
  • Thesis submission with declaration of originality + cover page per templates (Annexure 35–40).
  • Thesis evaluation (external review).
  • Public defense of thesis → Provisional Degree Certificate (PDC).
  • Upload full thesis text to Shodhganga.
  • PhD degree award & transcript.

! Things to Keep in Mind

  • Scholarship = 3.5 years from joining. No stipend for any extended period; return full amount if you discontinue or registration is cancelled.
  • No other salary/stipend from any source during the fellowship tenure.
  • No unrelated RA/JRF/SRF roles. You can't join another project outside your PhD research area.
  • Financial assistance cap: ₹30,000 total (registration/TA/DA/accommodation) — use within 4 years or before thesis, whichever is earlier. Apply via Annexure 48.
  • Leaves: 12 Casual Leave + 15 Special Casual Leave (SCL) per calendar year. SCL = conferences/workshops/seminars only. All leaves need prior approval from guide + HOD/HOI.
  • Physical attendance required for IPAC protocol presentation and every half-yearly DAC presentation.
  • Extra leave extends your duration (adds to minimum completion time). Maternity leave must be intimated to CDS (Annexure 43).
  • Switchover possible to a national fellowship (e.g., JRF/SRF) — needs CDS approval through guide/HOD/HOI (Annexure 47).
  • Registration date is your anchor. Everything in the system will hinge on it — set it the moment you get your registration letter.
  • Contingency ₹1.5L/yr for 3 yrs applies to National Fellowship holders — not TMA Pai. Don't plan on it. See the Funding section below.

Funding Grants & Financial Assistance

What you get, what it can be used for, and the limits
  • Dr. TMA Pai Scholarship (stipend) — your main grant-in-aid for the 3.5-year tenure.
    Rule: you may not accept additional salary/stipend from any other source while holding it. If you discontinue or your registration is cancelled, you must return the full scholarship amount received so far.
  • Financial Assistance — ₹30,000 cap (Registration / TA / DA / Accommodation) for the entire duration of the PhD.
    Use within 4 years of admission OR before thesis submission — whichever is earlier. Unused money is lost; plan to spend it deliberately.
  • What it can be used for: presenting a paper/poster at national or international conferences, and attending workshops/seminars.
    Not for general travel, equipment, or manpower — those aren't covered by this bucket.
  • Prior approval required. Apply via Annexure 48 with recommendations from your guide and HOI before you spend.
    Don't book travel and ask later — approval comes first.
  • SCL (15 days/yr) pairs with this. Conferences/workshops you fund through the ₹30K can use Special Casual Leave, with prior approval from guide + HOD/HOI.
⚠ Not for you (yet): the ₹1.5 lakh/year contingency grant (3 years) applies to the National Fellowship category — not Dr. TMA Pai. Don't budget for it. If you later switch to a national fellowship, the picture changes — revisit then.

Aware Other Important Things To Be Aware Of

Beyond the checklist — rules, caveats & what can go wrong
  • Coursework is mandatory & graded. Register right after joining; graded per the scheme (Annexure 30/31). Must be completed to stay on track — don't let it slide behind research.
  • Originality of work. Thesis & publications must be original; you sign an originality declaration (Annexure 35) and plagiarism checks apply. Cite everything in your literature notes early.
  • Duration cap = 6 years max. Extension beyond 3.5 is possible (Annexure 45) but the scholarship won't pay past 3.5 years. Aim to finish in 3.5–4.
  • Academic break exists (Annexure 44) — but a break extends your duration. Use only if you must.
  • Cancellation of registration is possible (non-payment, non-renewal, slow progress, misconduct) — and triggers scholarship repayment. Renew on time, every semester.
  • Re-registration for women scholars — a special provision if a woman scholar needs to pause/resume; check with CDS if relevant.
  • Maternity leave must be intimated to CDS (Annexure 43) — it extends the minimum duration.
  • Thesis title can be modified with CDS approval (Annexure 46) — you're not locked in to the working title.
  • Category switchover is possible — e.g. TMA Pai → national fellowship (JRF/SRF) with CDS approval up the chain (Annexure 47). This changes your funding picture.
  • Teaching / research assistantship may be available to PhD scholars (Section 14) — ask your guide/HOD if you want it.
  • Your guide & DAC are your anchor. Guide = primary mentor; DAC constituted ~month 1 sets your topic + coursework. Stay in regular touch.
  • Portals & profiles to know: candidate portal mahephd.azurewebsites.net, researcher.manipal.edu (guide profiles), and your learner email for all official comms.

Now Right Now — Before You Join

Practical steps while you wait for the joining email
  • Keep your originals safe — degree certificates, all marksheets, PAN & Aadhaar copies, passport photos. You'll need them for in-person verification at admission.
  • Watch for the joining email — start the joining-report procedure the moment it lands (the clock doesn't tick until you're registered, so no rush, but be ready).
  • Read your guide's recent lichen papers now — understand her data-collection & analysis approach before formalities begin (your own plan).
  • Confirm course fees & payment schedule on the candidate portal / with admissions so there are no payment surprises early on.
  • Set up the PhD management system now, so it's ready before the formalities start.

Content What Goes Inside The Formal Documents

These are big content milestones, not just boxes to tick
  • Research Protocol (IPAC, Annexure 25) — needs the problem, objectives, methodology, and expected outcomes. Your guide approves it before it reaches IPAC.
  • Half-yearly Progress Report (Annexure 28) + grading rubric (Annexure 29) — what you did, results, next steps. Feed it from your daily log so it writes itself.
  • Thesis format (Annexure 37–40) — prescribed sections, cover page, declaration, originality, and a word-count undertaking (Annexure 40). Follow these from your first draft so you don't reformat everything at the end.
  • Keep receipts & records for all ₹30K financial-assistance spending — reimbursement needs documentation.
