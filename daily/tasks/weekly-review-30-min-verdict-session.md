Sunday 18:00 IST. The workflow publishes the prep at 12:30 UTC; sit any time
that evening. 30 minutes, with pi in chat.

## Steps

1. Open https://phd-os.swatibio.workers.dev/review.html
2. Read **Week in numbers** — days logged, entries, tasks done, overdue. Nothing to decide here; it just orients you.
3. **What slipped** — for each missed/rolled-over item say one word: why. No fixing, just naming.
4. **#question pile** — for each: answer now, park with a date, or drop. Verdicts to pi; pi edits the vault.
5. **#idea pile** — same three verdicts: act, park, drop.
6. **Rough pile** (meeting fragments) — refine into meeting notes or leave; nothing auto-expires.
7. **Rolled-over tasks** — tick off the ones actually done; drop the dead ones. Dropping is a verdict, not a failure.
8. Done. Don't make up a missed Sunday — the conformance section records the gap; next Sunday starts fresh.
